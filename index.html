How many chunks?

MODELLINGWhiteboards or sticky notes · cold call
Mathematics

Find the gradient of the line through (2, 3) and (6, 11).

1How many chunks?
2One prerequisite skill or piece of knowledge?
Possible chunks
  • 1What gradient means: rise over run
  • 2Which number is x, which is y
  • 3Rise: 11 − 3 = 8
  • 4Run: 6 − 2 = 4
  • 5Subtract in the same order
  • 6Divide: 8 ÷ 4 = 2
  • 7Interpret a positive gradient

Prerequisites: reading coordinates · subtraction (including negatives) · simplifying fractions · the word “gradient”
